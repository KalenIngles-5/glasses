Help

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Write to us and a real optician will reply within one working day.

What if my frames do not fit?

Bring them back within 30 days for a free adjustment or exchange. We will also re-check your lenses at no cost.

How long does an order take?

Most single-vision orders are ready within five working days. Progressive lenses can take up to ten.

Do you accept my prescription from another optician?

Yes. We check every prescription twice before cutting the lenses, so please bring a copy or send it with your order.

Is the eye test really free?

Yes, with any frame purchase. Our eye test is done by a qualified optometrist and you will get a copy of your results.

What is covered by the warranty?

Frames and lens coatings are covered for two years against manufacturing faults. Accidental damage is repaired at a fixed price.
